# SnapLedger

Snap a photo of any receipt and SnapLedger turns it into a tidy expense entry.
Track monthly budgets, see where your money goes with simple charts, and get a gentle reminder when you are close to your limit.

## Features
- Scan paper receipts with the camera (on-device text recognition)
- Automatic category suggestions (groceries, dining, transport, ...)
- Monthly budget with progress bar and reminders
- Category and 6-month reports
- Sign in with Google to back up entries to the cloud
- Works offline; syncs when online

## Tech
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, CameraX, ML Kit Text Recognition, Room, Firebase Auth/Firestore, Google Mobile Ads.
